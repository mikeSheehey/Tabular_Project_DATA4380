## Current Research Question Ideas

### 1. Pitcher Profiles
**Which pitch characteristics best distinguish individual pitchers, and can these characteristics be used to identify distinct pitcher profiles?**

Possible features:
- Pitch velocity
- Spin rate
- Horizontal movement
- Vertical movement
- Pitch location
- Release characteristics

This could include PCA to reduce the large number of numerical features and identify the main patterns that differentiate pitchers.

---

### 2. Batter Swing Prediction
**Can pitch characteristics such as speed, movement, location, and game count be used to predict whether a batter will swing at a pitch?**

Possible task:
- Binary classification
- Swing vs. no swing

---

### 3. Pitch Outcome Prediction
**Can pitch characteristics be used to predict the outcome of a pitch?**

Possible outcomes:
- Called ball
- Called strike
- Swinging strike
- Foul
- Ball in play

---

### 4. Team Pitching Differences
**Which pitch characteristics best distinguish UTA pitching from South Dakota State pitching during the game?**

This could examine differences in:
- Speed
- Spin
- Movement
- Pitch location
- Other measurable pitching characteristics
